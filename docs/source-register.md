## Open Source Conflicts / Missing Evidence

- The Mini-B connector source mismatch is resolved: the copied MCU sheet, BOM,
  local symbol metadata, and PCB footprint now all use Würth `65100516121` /
  LCSC `C5120592`. USB ESD placement and final JLCPCB quote acceptance remain
  part of layout/procurement review.
- The current bench monitor-PD feedback circuit is not a universal 3-pin laser
  can interface. It is polarity-compatible with `D7805I`, `D6505I`, and
  `PLT5 520EB_P` in the 2026-06-28 Digikey cart, and the current `240R` /
  INA4180A1 gain-20 scaling covers the selected monitor-current range inside
  the local production ADC-headroom guard. D7805I 600 uA high-end monitor
  current maps to about 2.88 V. The 2026-07-04 direct-laser signoff closes the
  selected MPN/footprint pad-net mapping review for this PCB. It still cannot
  read monitor telemetry from `PLT5 450GB` because that part has no monitor
  photodiode, so `MPD_RAW4`/`MPD4` is spare/open for the selected blue source.
- The SFH2201/OPA380 signal-PD topology is connected, but the TIA readout is a
  high-sensitivity bench range, not a proven production optical range. At
  VBIAS = 1.5 V and RF = 2 MOhm the OPA380 headroom is only about
  +1.40 uA / -0.70 uA; the SFH2201 1000 lx short-circuit-current example would
  require about 152 V of TIA swing. Define the real Vivonics optical
  photocurrent range, shielding, RF/VBIAS settings, and AD7606 calibration
  before production use.
- SS14 BOM row uses JLCPCB/LCSC C2480. The 2026-07-04 source/order signoff
  confirms MDD(Microdiode Semiconductor) SS14, SMA(DO-214AC), and current PCB
  D5/D6 polarity. Recheck the JLCPCB quote/cart if C2480 is substituted.
- Commodity passive voltage ratings and steady-state resistor power are encoded
  in `check_passive_derating.py`, and the first-article passive AVL lock captures
  the current 24 MPN/LCSC pairs. Production still needs quote-time
  lifecycle/stock review, approved substitutions, pulse/surge/current derating,
  final orderable datasheets, and board-temperature evidence.
- The local AP632 capacitor recommendation guard now passes with C61+C62 VIN
  ceramic capacitance and 2x22 uF output banks on both buck rails. Production
  still needs ripple/stability/transient and buck-temperature measurements.
- Manufacturing class and fab tier are selected for this bench prototype:
  IPC/J-STD Class 2 workmanship target, JLCPCB Economic PCBA, 4-layer rigid
  FR-4, 1.6 mm standard stackup, top-side SMT assembly only, Gerber/drill upload
  from `circuits/laser_controller_gerbers.zip`, and BOM/CPL upload from
  `circuits/laser_controller_bom_jlcpcb.csv` plus
  `circuits/fab/laser_controller_pos.csv`. This selection does not waive quote
  review, KiCad ERC/DRC, or the remaining source/procurement blockers.
