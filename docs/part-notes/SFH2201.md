# SFH2201 Part Note

Source:
- ams OSRAM SFH 2201 A01 datasheet:
  `https://look.ams-osram.com/m/151c0967b1d4864e/original/SFH-2201-A01.pdf`

Datasheet limits used in the design:
- Reverse voltage maximum is 16 V; the bench signal-PD bias is 5 V.
- Sensitivity covers 300 nm to 1100 nm, matching the bench IR/RED/GREEN/BLUE
  source set.
- The datasheet's 1000 lx short-circuit-current example is 76 uA; at the
  current 2 MOhm TIA feedback setting that would require about 152 V of swing,
  so bright ambient or excessive optical power will saturate the bench TIA.

Pin checklist:
- Pin 1 is cathode.
- Pin 2 is anode.
- Datasheet package marking identifies cathode.

Current design:
- Cathode is reverse-biased from +5 V through 1 kOhm and locally bypassed.
- Anode goes to the OPA380 inverting summing node.
- The photodiode is used as the external/sample optical readout path, not as
  the internal laser monitor photodiode.

Layout notes:
- Place at optical edge and keep the anode/summing node extremely short.
- Avoid mechanical stress on the clear silicone body during assembly.

Checker evidence:
- Netlist checker asserts SFH2201 pin functions and photodiode TIA orientation.
- PCB checker enforces photodiode/TIA input-feedback-bias proximity.
- `check_tia_readout_budget.py` asserts the four SFH2201/OPA380 channels,
  checks 5 V reverse bias against the 16 V limit, and intentionally fails the
  SFH2201 1000 lx example to keep optical range/calibration visible.
