# SFH2201 Part Note

Source:
- ams OSRAM SFH 2201 A01 datasheet:
  `https://look.ams-osram.com/m/151c0967b1d4864e/original/SFH-2201-A01.pdf`

Pin checklist:
- Pin 1 is cathode.
- Pin 2 is anode.
- Datasheet package marking identifies cathode.

Current design:
- Cathode is reverse-biased from +5 V through 1 kOhm and locally bypassed.
- Anode goes to the OPA380 inverting summing node.
- The photodiode is used as the external/sample optical readout path, not as
  the internal laser monitor photodiode.

Layout notes:
- Place at optical edge and keep the anode/summing node extremely short.
- Avoid mechanical stress on the clear silicone body during assembly.

Checker evidence:
- Netlist checker asserts SFH2201 pin functions and photodiode TIA orientation.
- PCB checker enforces photodiode/TIA input-feedback-bias proximity.
