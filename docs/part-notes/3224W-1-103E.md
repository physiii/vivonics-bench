# Bourns 3224W Trimmer Part Note

Source:
- Bourns 3224 datasheet: `https://www.bourns.com/pdfs/3224.pdf`
- LCSC `C81348` order source for `3224W-1-103E` 10 k trimmer.
- LCSC `C116323` order source for `3224W-1-205E` 2 M trimmer.
- 2026-07-04 PCB orientation signoff:
  `circuits/review/signoff/2026-07-04-ss14-bourns-order-source-signoff.md`.

Pin/orientation signoff:
- Bourns identifies pin 2 as the wiper for the 3224 family.
- The active KiCad `Potentiometer_Bourns_3224W_Vertical` footprint uses pad 2
  as the large center pad and pads 1/3 as the end terminals.
- Current PCB extraction shows RV1-RV4 pad 2 on the `Net-(RVx-W)` VBIAS wiper
  nets, and RV5-RV8 pad 2 tied to `VOUT1..4`.
- RV5-RV8 pad 3 is also tied to `VOUT1..4`, so the 2 M feedback trimmers are
  wired as rheostats with the wiper tied to the OPA380 output side.

Current design:
- Used as the VBIAS trim element for each OPA380 channel.
- Series/bounding resistors prevent the non-inverting input from being trimmed
  outside the intended range.
- Used as the TIA feedback trim element for each OPA380 channel: `RV5..RV8`
  replace the prior fixed feedback resistors and sit in parallel with the local
  10 pF C0G feedback capacitor.
- Feedback trimmers are wired as rheostats with the wiper tied to the output
  side of the feedback network.
- The 2 MOhm feedback setting is a high-sensitivity, low-current range. The
  first-order TIA checker reports about +1.40 uA / -0.70 uA OPA380 headroom at
  VBIAS = 1.5 V, so the feedback trim value must be calibrated against the real
  Vivonics optical signal range before production.
- Bourns rates the 3224 family at 0.25 W at 85 C with 300 V maximum. The bench
  derating checker uses conservative full-5 V cases across the 10 k VBIAS trim
  element and the 2 M feedback trim element.

Layout notes:
- Wiper orientation signoff is captured in
  `circuits/review/signoff/2026-07-04-ss14-bourns-order-source-signoff.md`.
- Keep VBIAS routing quiet and away from switching traces.
- Keep RV5..RV8 tight to the OPA380 output/inverting-input feedback loop and
  away from laser-current switching paths.

Checker evidence:
- Netlist checker asserts VBIAS net signatures.
- Netlist checker asserts the feedback trim pin signatures.
- PCB extraction confirms RV1-RV8 pin 2 wiper orientation on the current board.
- PCB checker enforces VBIAS trim/passive proximity.
- `check_passive_derating.py` checks the trimmer's steady-state bench power and
  voltage stress.
