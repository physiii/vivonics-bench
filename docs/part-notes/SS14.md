# SS14 Part Note

Sources:
- LCSC C2480 BOM/order source.
- Vishay SS12-SS16 SMA family reference: `https://www.vishay.com/doc/?88746=`

Pin checklist:
- KiCad `D_SMA` pad 1 is anode.
- Pad 2 is cathode.

Current design:
- D5 anode receives USB `VBUS_5V`; D6 anode receives external `/POWER_IO/EXT5V`.
- D5/D6 cathodes OR into `+5V`.

Layout notes:
- Verify current and temperature in the final +5 V power tree.
- Confirm exact C2480 manufacturer datasheet and polarity at order.

Checker evidence:
- Netlist checker asserts D5/D6 anode/cathode nets.
- PCB/release gate asserts generated +5 V trunk connectivity; final KiCad DRC,
  zone refill, and visual +5 V/GND return review are still required.
