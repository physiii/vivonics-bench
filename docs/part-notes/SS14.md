Order-source signoff:
- JLCPCB and LCSC identify C2480 as MDD(Microdiode Semiconductor) `SS14`,
  package `SMA(DO-214AC)`, 40 V / 1 A Schottky diode.
- JLCPCB lists C2480 as a Basic SMT Assembly part.

Pin checklist:
- KiCad `D_SMA` pad 1 is anode.
- Pad 2 is cathode.

Current design:
- D5 anode receives USB `VBUS_5V`; D6 anode receives onboard AP63205 `/POWER_IO/BUCK_5V`.
- D5/D6 cathodes OR into `+5V`.

Layout notes:
- Verify current and temperature in the final +5 V power tree.
- Recheck the JLCPCB quote/cart if C2480 is substituted.

Checker evidence:
- Netlist checker asserts D5/D6 anode/cathode nets.
- 2026-07-04 order-source signoff confirms current C2480 manufacturer/package
  identity and current PCB D5/D6 polarity.
- PCB/release gate asserts generated +5 V trunk connectivity; final KiCad DRC,
  zone refill, and visual +5 V/GND return review are still required.
