# AO3400A Part Note

Source:
- Alpha & Omega AO3400A datasheet:
  `https://www.aosmd.com/res/data_sheets/AO3400A.pdf`
  - The official AOS PDF is reachable from the current source-document probe;
    release-time manual latest-revision verification remains required.

Pin checklist:
- SOT-23 pin 1 = gate.
- Pin 2 = source.
- Pin 3 = drain.

Current design:
- Gate is driven from TLV9001 output through 1 kOhm.
- Source returns through a 10 ohm 2512 sense resistor.
- Drain connects to the direct laser cathode net `LASER_Nx`.
- The AO3400A is used as a linear low-side current-sink pass device here, not
  merely as a saturated switch. Its heat is `I * (LASER_V+ - Vf(laser) -
  I*10 ohm)`.
- At the selected 120 mA maximum-current check point, the 10 ohm sense node is
  1.200 V and the TLV9001 has about 3.780 V of available AO3400A gate-source
  drive.
- At the raw 247.5 mA hardware clamp, the sense node is 2.475 V and the same
  light-load TLV9001 output-high assumption leaves only about 0.005 V above the
  AO3400A 2.5 V RDS(on) characterization point. That clamp is not a production
  operating point.

Layout notes:
- Treat drain/source/sense routing as current path, not ordinary small signal.
- Thermal/SOA must be checked against actual `LASER_V+`, selected laser diode
  forward voltage, duty cycle, and programmed current.
- AO3400A thermal impedance and safe-operating-area behavior are application
  board dependent; final copper, duty cycle, and measured case/board
  temperature must close the release decision.

Checker evidence:
- Netlist checker asserts AO3400A pin functions and laser cathode/source/gate
  nets.
- `check_laser_driver_package_pcb.py` asserts Q1-Q4 AO3400A gate/source/drain
  schematic pin nets, current PCB pad-net assignments, local gate/sense
  component nets, and installed KiCad `SOT-23` pad geometry.
- PCB checker reviews laser-current route geometry and class membership.
- `check_laser_driver_control_loop.py` checks AO3400A gate/source/drain
  topology, the selected-max-current gate-drive budget, and the
  `hardware-clamp-gate-margin` expected-fail case.
- `check_laser_current_budget.py` checks the AO3400A dissipation for the selected
  diode forward-voltage and `LASER_V+` assumption.
- `selected-diodes-typ-10v72` preserves the old 10.72 V expected-fail comparison
  for PLT5 450GB; `selected-diodes-max-9v3` is the passing present
  current-limited common-rail reference; `selected-diodes-hardware-clamp-10v72`
  fails because 247.5 mA is above every selected diode operating-current max.
