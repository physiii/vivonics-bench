# AP63200 / AP63205 Buck Part Note

Sources:
- Diodes Inc. `AP63200/AP63201/AP63203/AP63205` datasheet:
  `https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/AP63200-AP63201-AP63203-AP63205.pdf`
- Sunlord `MWSA0503S-4R7MT` inductor source:
  `https://file.huaqiu.com/web2/M00/8E/7A/poYBAGPgp_6AQk-KADb9nDF4tUU137.pdf`
- Sunlord `WPN4020H100MT` inductor source:
  `https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/Sunlord-WPN4020H100MT/C98364`

Package and pin checklist:
- `AP63205WU-7` is U15 in TSOT-23-6 and generates `/POWER_IO/BUCK_5V`.
- `AP63200WU-7` is U16 in TSOT-23-6 and generates `LASER_V+`.
- TSOT-23-6 pin 1 is `FB`.
- Pin 2 is `EN`.
- Pin 3 is `IN`.
- Pin 4 is `GND`.
- Pin 5 is `SW`.
- Pin 6 is `BST`.
- Both buck `IN` and `EN` pins are tied to `VIN_24V`, so both rails are on
  whenever the 24 V barrel/RJ45 input is present.
- Each `BST` pin uses a local `100nF BST` capacitor to its `SW` node.
- `check_ap6320x_package_pcb.py` asserts the exported U15/U16 schematic pin
  nets, current PCB pad nets, KiCad TSOT-23-6 pad geometry, and the local L1/L2
  inductor footprint pad geometry.

Current design:
- U15 `AP63205WU-7` is the fixed 5 V member. Its `FB` pin is tied to
  `/POWER_IO/BUCK_5V`, `SW` drives L1, and L1 is `MWSA0503S-4R7MT`.
- U16 `AP63200WU-7` is the adjustable member. Its `FB` pin is the midpoint of
  R61/R62/C69, `SW` drives L2, and L2 is `WPN4020H100MT`.
- R61/R62 are `237k` / `22.1k`, so the AP63200 feedback equation computes
  `0.8 V * (1 + 237k/22.1k) = 9.38 V`.
- C69 is the `100pF FF` feed-forward capacitor from `LASER_V+` to the FB node.
- The AP632 family input range is `3.8 V` to `32 V`. A 24 V nominal rail leaves
  only 8 V margin to absolute input maximum before any adapter, harness,
  hot-plug, or inductive transient is considered.
- The datasheet layout notes call for close VIN capacitors, close feedback
  parts, sufficient GND-side capacitor vias, and a large GND plane under the
  part for thermal dissipation.

Bench current policy:
- `check_buck_input_power_budget.py --policy bench-selected-max-9v3` checks a
  reduced 9.3 V laser-rail reference, selected laser max currents, and a 350 mA
  +5 V load. It stays below the 500 mA J5 barrel connector bench rating.
- `check_buck_input_power_budget.py --policy present-typ-9v3` checks input and
  inductor current for the present 9.3 V feedback setting at selected laser
  typical currents. Laser AO3400A thermal remains a separate failing policy for
  PLT5 450GB.
- `check_buck_input_power_budget.py --policy hardware-clamp-9v3` checks the
  all-channel per-channel analog-limit case after the limiter update. It now
  stays below the 500 mA barrel connector bench input rating, but it does not
  replace bench ripple, transient, stability, or temperature measurements.
- `check_vin24_input_protection.py --policy bench-topology` asserts the current
  J5/J6-to-`VIN_24V` topology: J5 pin 1 and J6 pins 4/5 feed `VIN_24V`, J5
  pins 2/3 and J6 pins 7/8/9/11 return to GND, and U15/U16 IN/EN pins are on
  the same `VIN_24V` net.

Production blocker:
- `check_vin24_input_protection.py --policy production-protection` is expected
  to fail until the 24 V entry includes a defined current limit/fuse/PTC,
  reverse-polarity strategy, transient suppression/TVS decision, and adapter/RJ45
  harness rating. The present schematic has no fuse/PTC/TVS/reverse-protection
  or eFuse/hot-swap component in the VIN path.
- `check_buck_input_power_budget.py --policy datasheet-recommended-components`
  now passes the local AP632 capacitor identity/count guard with 2x22 uF output
  banks on both buck rails. Bench ripple, transient, stability, and thermal
  measurements are still required before treating the buck rails as production
  released.
- Current VIN ceramic capacitance is C61+C62 = `20uF` plus C70 `22uF`
  electrolytic, so the local checker no longer flags the generic AP632 input
  ceramic threshold.
- Current buck output capacitance is C64+C65 = `44uF` on `/POWER_IO/BUCK_5V`
  and C67+C68 = `44uF` on `LASER_V+`, using 2x22 uF 25 V 0805 ceramics per
  rail.
- The 24 V nominal input uses 80 percent of the J5 30 V barrel voltage rating
  and 75 percent of the AP632 32 V input maximum before adapter, harness,
  hot-plug, or inductive transients.
