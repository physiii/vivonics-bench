# AP2112K-3.3TRG1 Part Note

Sources:
- Diodes AP2112 product page: `https://www.diodes.com/part/view/AP2112`
- Diodes AP2112 datasheet: `https://www.diodes.com/assets/Datasheets/AP2112.pdf`
- Espressif ESP32-S3-WROOM-1/WROOM-1U datasheet:
  `https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-s3-wroom-1_wroom-1u_datasheet_en.pdf`
- Espressif ESP32-S3 series datasheet:
  `https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-s3_datasheet_en.pdf`

Pin checklist:
- SOT25 pin 1 = VIN.
- Pin 2 = GND.
- Pin 3 = EN.
- Pin 4 = NC.
- Pin 5 = VOUT.

Current design:
- VIN and EN are tied to +5 V.
- VOUT creates +3V3 for the ESP32-S3 module, EN/BOOT pull-ups, and decoupling.
- Pin 4 is deliberate no-connect.
- The bench policy is USB/UART control with ESP32 RF disabled. Under that policy,
  continuous +3V3 load is capped at 120 mA unless a measured rail-current and
  temperature result proves more margin.

Thermal budget:
- AP2112 SOT25 thetaJA is 184 degC/W with no heatsink. The 5 V to 3.3 V
  dissipation is approximately `(5.0 V - 3.3 V) * Iout + 5.0 V * Iq`.
- At 120 mA continuous load and 85 degC ambient, calculated junction temperature
  is about 123 degC using `Iq(max)=80 uA`. This is acceptable only as a
  bench/no-RF policy against a 125 degC design target, and still needs bring-up
  temperature measurement.
- Espressif lists ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 RF peaks of 355 mA for 802.11b TX at
  100 percent duty cycle and 344 mA for Bluetooth LE TX at 20 dBm. Those loads
  fail this AP2112 SOT25 thermal budget from a 5 V source.
- Production or sustained Wi-Fi/BLE use should replace this rail with a buck
  regulator or a larger thermally proven regulator solution.

Layout notes:
- Keep input/output capacitors close to AP2112.
- Keep copper around VIN, VOUT, and GND available for heat spreading where it
  does not violate antenna, USB, or analog return constraints.

Checker evidence:
- Netlist checker asserts AP2112 pin names/functions and exact rail membership.
- PCB checker enforces AP2112 input/output capacitor proximity and explicit
  +3V3 local route connectivity.
- `check_power_thermal_budget.py --policy bench-uart-usb` encodes the accepted
  bench/no-RF thermal policy; `--policy wifi-tx-100-duty` is an intentional
  fail case for sustained RF on this LDO.
- `circuits/review/signoff/2026-07-05-ap2112-first-article-signoff.md` records
  the first-article no-RF operating limit and the remaining temperature/current
  measurement requirement.
